OceanGuardian AI
Protecting Oceans Through Intelligent Conservation
OceanGuardian AI is an AWS-powered multi-agent conservation intelligence platform designed to help governments, NGOs, researchers, and environmental organizations identify marine protection gaps, assess threats, simulate conservation scenarios, and generate actionable recommendations.
