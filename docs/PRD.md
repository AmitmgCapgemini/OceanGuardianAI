OceanGuardian AI
Problem Statement
Marine conservation organizations face challenges in analyzing fragmented biodiversity, protection, climate, and human activity datasets.

Identifying priority conservation areas requires combining multiple sources and performing geospatial analysis that is often difficult and time consuming.

Objective
Build an AI-powered conservation intelligence platform that helps users:

Explore marine datasets
Identify protection gaps
Analyze environmental threats
Simulate conservation scenarios
Generate explainable recommendations
Target Users
WWF
Identify marine conservation priorities.

Governments
Evaluate Marine Protected Area expansion.

NGOs
Assess ecosystem risks and intervention opportunities.

Researchers
Explore biodiversity and protection datasets.

Core Features
Feature 1
Marine Intelligence Map

Feature 2
AI Conservation Assistant

Feature 3
Protection Gap Analysis

Feature 4
Scenario Simulation

Feature 5
Executive Report Generator

Success Criteria
Users can identify high-value conservation areas and evaluate protection strategies using AI-driven insights and geospatial analysis.
