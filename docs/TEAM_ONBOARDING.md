Project Vision

OceanGuardian AI is an AI-powered marine conservation intelligence platform designed to help conservation organizations:

Identify marine protection opportunities
Understand protection gaps
Analyze conservation risks
Explore conservation scenarios
Generate actionable recommendations

The solution leverages AWS services, AI, and marine conservation datasets.
